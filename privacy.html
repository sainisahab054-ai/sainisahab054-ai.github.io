Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 October 2026

This policy explains how the SS Report Master apps (the Windows apps and the Android app, together "the App") handle information. The App is a private business tool used by staff of the businesses that set it up.

1. Information the App uses

  • Google account access (Android app): when you sign in with Google, the App receives permission to access Google Drive (scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive) and your email address.
  • Google Drive data: the App only works inside the shared report folders that you choose in the App (folders containing Requests, Responses and Status). It reads the report list and status files, creates small request files, downloads the report files prepared for you, and deletes its own request files afterwards. It does not read, change or delete any other files in your Google Drive. It also lists folder names when you search for the shared folder to add.
  • Information you enter: your name (shown in the office request log), server names, report choices and dates, and the server PIN.
  • Device name: the phone model or computer name is included in each request so the office can see which device asked for a report.

2. How the information is used

The information is used only to send your report requests to your office server and to save the reports on your device. Access tokens and any remembered PIN (stored only as a one-way hash) are kept on your device.

3. Sharing

The App has no server of its own, does not use advertising or analytics, and does not sell, rent or share your information with anyone. Request files are visible only to the people who can open the shared Google Drive folder, which is controlled by your office.

4. Google API Services User Data Policy

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the report features described above, is not transferred to others except as needed for those features or required by law, is not used for advertising, and is not read by humans except with your consent or for security or legal reasons.

5. Storage and retention

Reports you download are saved in your device's Download/SS Report Master folder until you delete them. Request and response files in the shared folder are removed automatically after a few days by the office server.

6. Your choices

  • Remove the App's Google access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Clear the App's data or uninstall it to delete the settings stored on your device.
  • Ask your office to stop sharing the Drive folder with you.

7. Children

The App is a business tool and is not intended for children.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated. The effective date above shows the latest version.
